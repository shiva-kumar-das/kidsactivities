Lesson 1

👀 Understand before you judge

You see a child holding another child's toy. It looks like he took it.
What should you do FIRST?
Lesson 2

❓ Ask “Why?”

A friend suddenly pushes another child. What wise question could you ask?
Lesson 3

🔄 Actions can have different reasons

Two children take an apple. One takes it without asking. The other takes it because the teacher asked him to bring it.
Do both actions mean exactly the same thing?
Lesson 4

⚖️ Justice needs truth

The king has heard only one side of the story. Is he ready to decide?
Lesson 5

📜 Rules and circumstances

A child breaks a rule because there is an emergency and someone needs help.
Should we ignore all rules?
Lesson 6

❤️ Compassion and responsibility

The man was hungry. Does that automatically mean stealing was the best solution?
Lesson 7

🔍 Look beyond the person

The king has found the man. What bigger question should he ask?
Lesson 8

🌱 Find the root cause

The king gives this family food today. But tomorrow another family becomes hungry.
What could the king think about?
Lesson 9

🫶 Protect everyone

Whose needs should the king think about?
Lesson 10

⚖️❤️ Justice + Compassion

Can a wise decision care about someone's suffering AND still be fair?
Lesson 11

🚫🏷️ Don't label too quickly

You see someone make a mistake. You do not know what happened before it.
What could you say?
Lesson 12

💡 More than one solution

The king wants to help the family, be fair to the baker, and protect the kingdom. Can he think of more than one helpful action?

The King's Wisdom

🌟 You became a thinking adviser!

STOP → LOOK → ASK WHY → UNDERSTAND → THINK → DECIDE

A wise person does not rush to judge. A wise person looks deeper, listens, understands, cares about everyone, and searches for a fair solution.
